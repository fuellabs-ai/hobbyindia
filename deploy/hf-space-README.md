# HobbyIndia

An independent guide by Fuel Labs. Not a government website.

Type your problem in your own words (English, Hinglish, Tanglish or Kanglish). HobbyLM-Embed
(`harims95/hobbylm-embed-140m`) matches it to a fixed card or decision flow written by people,
and sends you to the official government page. Nothing is generated. No login. Questions are
not logged or stored.

Emergency: call 112. Cyber fraud: call 1930 or report at https://cybercrime.gov.in/

Copy this file to the Space repo as README.md, next to the Dockerfile.
